A project manager is managing a project where stakeholders frequently request scope changes. What should the project manager do first?

  • A. Reject the change
  • B. Escalate to the sponsor
  • C. Follow the change control process
  • D. Update the schedule
Correct Answer: C — Follow the change control process

The project manager should evaluate the change request through the formal change control process before accepting or rejecting it. This ensures proper impact analysis on scope, schedule, and cost.

A team member reports a risk that could delay the project. What should the project manager do next?

  • A. Ignore the risk
  • B. Add it to the risk register
  • C. Escalate immediately
  • D. Change the schedule
Correct Answer: B — Add it to the risk register

All identified risks should be recorded in the risk register and assessed for probability and impact before mitigation actions are taken.
